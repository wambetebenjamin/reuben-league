## Template imagery

`public/images/template/` keeps the photographs that shipped with the uploaded Stamina template (Free-Template.co,
licensed CC BY 3.0). They are retained as reference assets from the zip and are not loaded by any page. `bg_1.jpg`,
`bg_2.jpg` and `bg_3.jpg` are unmodified copies of the originals.

## Icons and fonts

- Interface icons: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC license), installed through `lucide-react`.
- Muli typeface: SIL Open Font License, self hosted from `public/fonts/muli/`, matching the template's Google Fonts
  request for Muli 300, 400, 700 and 900.
- Flaticon and icomoon icon fonts: font files and stylesheets copied from the uploaded template
  (`public/fonts/flaticon`, `public/fonts/icomoon`). The template's font demo pages and `selection.json` are left in
  the zip rather than deployed.
